Professional Experience

Inmates Welfare Officer (Captain) 2016 — Present
Reform, Rehabilitation & Pretrial Detention Centre for Women · Ministry of Interior, Bahrain

Oversee the psychological, social, and rehabilitative wellbeing of female inmates, serving as the centre's principal liaison for welfare programming and inter-agency coordination.

  • Conceived, coordinated, and led the Psychological & Social Support Program for Female Inmates in strategic partnership with the Royal Charity Organisation for Humanitarian Works — initiating coordination in 2024 and launching full implementation in November 2025.
  • Directed weekly in-centre counselling sessions delivered jointly with the Head of Family Counselling at the Royal Charity Organisation, structured around self-acceptance, social insight, stress management, behavioural change, family support, and future planning.
  • Selected and managed a cohort of 20 inmates for structured group and individual sessions based on individualised psychological and social needs assessments, and extended continuity of care to 2 cases following release.
  • Organised complementary wellbeing activities — fitness sessions, workshops, and interactive engagement programmes — and delegated supervised responsibilities to participants to build accountability and life skills.
  • Achieved and sustained a session attendance rate above 80%, contributing to measurable improvements in participants' psychological wellbeing, self-confidence, and family relationships.
  • Authored programme recommendations to extend aftercare services in partnership with the Royal Charity Organisation's "Ma'akum" initiative, supporting sustainable reintegration and reduced recidivism for released inmates.
